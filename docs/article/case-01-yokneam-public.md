# Case file No. 1 — Yokneam Illit, the night of 26–27 October 2026. Public summary

ElectionForensics project, case `2026-10-26-yokneam-opposition-posters-removed` (No. 1), opened 28 October 2026. This
summary is part of the replication package of the article «Seats at Stake: A Conditional Estimate of the Knesset Seats
Movable by a One-Sided Replacement of Outdoor Campaign Advertising, Israel 2026» and is reproduced in it as Appendix H
together with the photographs. The photographs are published in full (§ 4); the investigator's protocol and the
verbatim witness statement with its two addenda are retained by the editor and are not released. This document states
what the file contains and nothing more; where the article refers to the case, it refers to the facts listed here.

## 1. What the file is

- **The witness** is the editor of the article, Raveh Neeman: a first-hand witness and a participant in the events — he
  hung the posters with two helpers. His interest is stated in the article's Editor statement: active for the opposition
  in Yokneam's campaigns since 2019, a member of the Yesh Atid party centre until the summer of 2026, no party membership
  now.
- **The statement** was given in writing on 28 October 2026 in answer to the questionnaire of the project's investigator
  protocol and recorded verbatim, without editing; two addenda of the same day answered follow-up questions by number.
  The witness's statement is the only testimony in the file: the two helpers were not interviewed (the witness's
  decision, recorded in the file).
- **Evidence grade** on the project's scale: **D** — the verbatim statement of a named eyewitness with no independent
  source. The grade describes the independence of the sources, not the strength of the inference; it rises to C with an
  independent source (a media report, a municipal record, an unconnected eyewitness) and to A with an official document.
- **Attribution.** The attribution of the removal to Likud teams is the witness's inference from two observations (Likud
  material appeared where the removed material had been; people in the streets spoke with the team). Nobody on the team
  saw the act of removal. The file records the attribution as `claimed`.

## 5. Other persons and episodes recorded in the file

- At עמק השלום 94 an unknown man approached the team and told them he was removing Netanyahu's posters; the witness told
  him that destroying campaign material is unlawful. Later, at הגיא 11, the same man removed a Netanyahu poster in front
  of a group of youths and their adult leader, who had been talking with the team; the leader described himself as
  non-partisan. The witness regards the two acts as a staged provocation meant to create an impression of mutual
  violations; the file records that as his interpretation and the two acts as what was observed. No time, description
  or vehicle is recorded; whether the call to the police mentioned the man is not recorded; the file makes no finding
  about him.
- At 01:32 a person watching the team was photographed (§ 4, Nos. 16–19); nothing more is known about him.

## 7. Qualification recorded in the file (preliminary)

What is observed is the disappearance of several lists' advertising at five polling sites across the town within two to
three hours on the night before the vote, with one list's material in its place at the four sites the team had
covered. As an offence this is § 126(2) of the Knesset Elections Law (removing, destroying or defacing a notice carrying
the propaganda of an approved list; the offence does not depend on whether the notice was lawfully placed) and
§§ 384 and 452 of the Penal Law; the synchrony suggests organisation, but no organiser is established. The town's own
2022→2026 swing is in line with comparable towns (a descriptive comparison, not a causal estimate; article, § 5). The
question of § 86 of the Elections Law — whether a defect could have affected the results — is not answered by this
case; it is the subject of the article's conditional computation, which does not use the case as an input.

## 8. Relation to the article

The article's §§ 1 and 2.1 state the case as it is here and not more: five sites — four of the six where the team had
hung posters and a fifth where it had not; one witness; the «after» photographed at one site; Likud material on the
freed places and the call to the police at 02:18 on the witness's account. The conditional computation of §§ 3–4 takes
no input from the case.
